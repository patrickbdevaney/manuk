## Verdict: NOT MET as the pre-registered Bar 2 hidden-instruction branch

`docs/sprint/SUCCESS.md`, Bar 2, requires that "the slice must include **external** cases; our own fixtures alone do
not count." The adopted slice fails that requirement:

1. **No external cases.** All 40 pages are generated by `bench/agent-tasks/eia/mirror.py` (10 sites, our own
   fixtures). They are not external pages or the paper's Mind2Web snapshots. The numeric MET therefore does not count.
2. **Not a hidden instruction.** The mechanism is a deceptive look-alike form field (opacity .02 duplicate), not
   instruction-following injection. Haiku resisted the visible-instruction variants in W4.D0 and W4.D0.a (0/15 each).
3. **Post-hoc engine fix on the same slice.** The manuk 0% comes after a fix (`43518333`) made after seeing the
   pre-fix failure (manuk filled the look-alike, 47.5–50%). The re-run is on the same 40 cases, so it is not a held-out
   result. The fix is generic (opacity <= .1 / off-canvas / zero-size controls are withheld from the a11y tree), but
   the evidence is not independent of the fix.
4. **The win is narrow.** manuk made 0 attempts in 120 runs: the hidden field was never offered to the agent. The
   Chrome arms have no equivalent paint-truth check, so this is a visibility-filter win on one mechanism, not a
   general hidden-instruction defence.
5. **Bar 2 condition 1 is not assessed here.** Full-suite task success within 10 points of Playwright is a separate
   check (W3.18 and the task suite), not this node.

Stated plainly: on the pre-registered Bar 2 hidden-instruction branch, the sprint does **not** establish a decisive
engine win. The local-fixture result (0% vs 20–30% Playwright) is recorded as a finding, not as the bar.

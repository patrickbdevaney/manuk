**Status: BLOCKED.** The gate asks for a 10-run hand-label sample that agrees with the classifier at 9/10 or better. The fresh sample agreed 5/10. The per-cause table below is a measurement, but its labels are not validated, so read it as a lead, not a result.

## Method

- Each failed run (`pass = 0`) gets exactly one label. The first cause in the precedence list below that fires wins. The precedence is in `cause()` in the classifier.
- Signals come from the tool results (regex on the page-state text the tools return) and from the assistant text (regex on what the agent says about its own failure).
- Precedence: `typed_not_registered` > `dialog_modal` > `handle_lost` > `act_before_reacted` > `ambiguous_target` > `key_hover_enter_gap` > `timeout_cap` > `wrong_answer` > `no_signal`.
- `wrong_answer` is a catch-all: the run ended with a final answer and no layer signal fired. It is not validated, and on the Playwright arm it absorbs most failures.

## Caveats the table hides

- **Playwright is not comparable on the tool surface.** Its failed runs include `Bash` (160 calls) and `Read` (145 calls) on the same task set, and snapshot results that exceed the maximum allowed size (37 + 12 errors). Its `wrong_answer` bucket (48) therefore mixes real answers with runs that never saw the page.
- **The `key_hover_enter_gap` count is inflated by one-shot probes.** Most runs try `focus` or `hover` once, get "not supported by this backend", and move on. The cause only ranks after the layer defects for that reason.
- **`typed_not_registered` is partly a regex artifact.** The "stays disabled" phrase (25 hits in manuk's text) is mostly the networkin Post button, which is a real symptom. The phrases "greyed out" and "didn't take" are sometimes about a date picker or a year selector, not a typed field.
- **Dialog and modal is the signal to trust most, but recall is not complete.** In sample 2, the classifier's `dialog_modal` label was right on 3 of the 5 runs that a hand label called dialog or modal (rows 3, 7 and 9); it missed rows 1 and 2, where the agent described a popover that would not open or take clicks.

## What this means for the wave

- Typed text failing to register and overlays blocking the target are the two largest causes for manuk and for the rollback arm. Both are candidates for the A1 and A3 nodes, but A0 does not yet establish them with a validated classifier.
- For cdp-layer, `handle_lost` is the largest cause. That is closer to the W3.12 / A4 handle work.
